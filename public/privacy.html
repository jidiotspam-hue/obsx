Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · Operated by Alexis Grimmace (individual operator of obsx.tech). This policy describes what data OBSX processes, how long it is kept, which strictly necessary cookies are used, and how to exercise your rights.

1. Operator & Contact Information

For the hosted demonstration and dashboard at obsx.tech, the data controller is Alexis Grimmace (individual operator), reachable at security@obsx.tech. If you connect to a third-party or self-hosted OBSX node operated by another organization, that operator is the data controller for that deployment.

2. Data Processed & Purpose

  • Attestation & Anti-Automation Telemetry (/api/v1/session, /api/v1/signal, /api/v1/verify): Request timestamp, masked/hashed network address, IP subnet prefix (IPv4 /24, IPv6 /48), User-Agent string, request header presence hints, SHA-256 Proof-of-Work challenge and nonce, descriptor integrity flags, and coarse hashed graphics capability tokens (sw_rasterizer or gpu_hw_<hash>; unhashed WebGL renderer strings are not persisted).
  • Origin Flood Shield (lib/botshield.js): Short-lived rate counters, challenge nonces, strike counters, and subnet/IP quarantine records used strictly to mitigate Layer-7 floods and abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest in network and service security).
  • Dashboard Account & Demo Notes (/api/v1/auth/email, /api/v1/project, /api/v1/notes): Email address (indexed by SHA-256 hash), project names and API keys created by you, demonstration note titles/contents, and timestamp/version records of your Terms & Privacy clickwrap acceptance (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) contract/service delivery).

3. Retention, Ephemeral Deletion & Disk Persistence

  • Immediate Post-Verification Scrub: Session telemetry and kinematic interaction records held in tr:s:<id> are deleted immediately upon completion of verify().
  • Bounded In-Memory TTLs: Unverified challenges (tr:c:*) expire after 300 seconds; unverified idle sessions expire after up to 3,900 seconds; single-use replay locks (tr:used:*) expire after the token replay window (up to 300 seconds); email one-time verification codes (tr:ec:*) expire after 600 seconds; authenticated dashboard sessions expire after 7 days.
  • Optional Disk Snapshot (.kv-snapshot.json): When OBSX_PERSIST_KV=true, non-sensitive state is serialized atomically with restricted file permissions (0600). Secret keys, one-time codes (tr:ec:*, tr:otp:*), auth tokens, and session behavioral signatures are explicitly scrubbed before disk writes.
  • Public Live Telemetry Feed: The telemetry table on / reflects observed events restricted strictly to the public sandbox project (pk_obsx_sandbox) with masked IP addresses and redacted internal diagnostics. Customer tenant events are never published.

4. Strictly Necessary Cookies & Zero External Calls

OBSX does not set advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies, and loads no external fonts, third-party scripts, or external telemetry trackers from your browser. When applicable, the origin uses only strictly necessary first-party cookies (with HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and __Host- / Secure attributes over HTTPS):

  • __Host-obsx_session / obsx_session: Authenticates signed-in dashboard and Notes sessions (7-day TTL).
  • obsx_bs: Records completion of the origin shield Proof-of-Work challenge (~15-minute TTL) so valid browsers are not re-challenged on every page request.
  • obsx_guest: Random pseudonymous partition identifier isolating anonymous Notes created in the public demo across visitors sharing an IP address or NAT.

5. Third-Party Transfers & Email Dispatch

OBSX makes zero automatic outbound data transmissions to external third parties during normal verification. On self-hosted deployments, if an operator configures SMTP credentials (SMTP_HOST, SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASS), one-time verification codes are delivered via that configured SMTP relay; operator incident alerts are sent only when an operator explicitly configures recipient addresses (ALERT_EMAIL / OPERATOR_EMAIL). Optional GitHub update polling (OBSX_AUTO_UPDATE) is disabled by default.

6. Automated Processing & Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

OBSX evaluates automated risk scores (0–100) strictly to protect protected endpoints from automated abuse (GDPR Art. 22(2) / legitimate security necessity). We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising under CCPA/CPRA.

  • Access & Portability (GDPR Art. 15, 20 / CCPA Know): Signed-in users can download a JSON archive of their profile, site keys, and notes anytime via Export My Data in the Dashboard (GET /api/v1/me/export).
  • Erasure (GDPR Art. 17 / CCPA Delete): Signed-in users can permanently delete their account, associated site keys, and notes directly from the Dashboard (DELETE /api/v1/me).
  • Human Review & Complaints: To request manual review of an automated block or submit a privacy inquiry, email security@obsx.tech. EU/UK residents also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.
